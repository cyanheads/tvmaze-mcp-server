# 0.3.0 — 2026-10-16

## Added

- **`tvmaze_get_person`** searches names into candidates and pages a selected person's cast, guest, or crew credits with compact show, episode, and character references; candidates are never selected automatically. ([#7](https://github.com/cyanheads/tvmaze-mcp-server/issues/7))
- **`tvmaze_get_schedule.channel`** filters exact channel names case-insensitively before paging, with filtered totals and bounded guidance when nothing matches. ([#17](https://github.com/cyanheads/tvmaze-mcp-server/issues/17))
- **`tvmaze_get_cast.name`** filters performer, character, and requested crew names before paging on both show and episode scopes. ([#18](https://github.com/cyanheads/tvmaze-mcp-server/issues/18))
- **`tvmaze_get_show`** adds optional `schedule_timezone` from the selected channel beside its recurring slot time; unavailable timezones remain absent. ([#19](https://github.com/cyanheads/tvmaze-mcp-server/issues/19))

## Changed

- **List limits** above 250 clamp to 250, reporting the requested limit, applied cap, and actual returned count; continuation still uses the current call's limit. ([#16](https://github.com/cyanheads/tvmaze-mcp-server/issues/16))
- **IMDb lookup** accepts uppercase prefixes, bare digits, and exact IMDb title URLs, preserving leading zeroes and echoing the canonical id. ([#21](https://github.com/cyanheads/tvmaze-mcp-server/issues/21))
- **Runtime and metadata** align on Bun 1.4.2 → 1.4.3, eight tools, and people/credits descriptions; bundled framework guidance is synchronized.

## Fixed

- **Text rendering** preserves literal Markdown punctuation in names, labels, headings, notices, and season-table cells while retaining synopsis emphasis and raw structured strings and URLs. ([#20](https://github.com/cyanheads/tvmaze-mcp-server/issues/20))
- **Required show titles** are trimmed; whitespace-only search queries and next-episode titles fail locally before fetching. ([#21](https://github.com/cyanheads/tvmaze-mcp-server/issues/21))
